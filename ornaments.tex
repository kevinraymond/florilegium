\begin{document}
\florilegiumornament{leaf}\enspace A paragraph can open with one leaf the size of a
letter, set with \code{\textbackslash florilegiumornament\{leaf\}}. Each ornament in a
document gets a shape of its own,
\florilegiumornament{leaf}\,\florilegiumornament{leaf}\,\florilegiumornament{leaf}\,\florilegiumornament{leaf},
and keeps it from one build to the next; \code{seed=random} draws a new one every time.

\florilegiumornament[size=2.4em]{leaf}\enspace\code{size=2.4em} makes it larger, \code{ink=5B2A1F} paints it in
another color \florilegiumornament[ink=5B2A1F]{leaf}\,\florilegiumornament[ink=2F6B4F]{leaf}\,\florilegiumornament[ink=8E1B3A]{leaf}\,\florilegiumornament[ink=4B3B8F]{leaf},
and \code{angle=30} turns it.

Any primitive will do:
\florilegiumornament{flower}\,\florilegiumornament{blossom}\,\florilegiumornament{bud}\,\florilegiumornament{bell}\,%
\florilegiumornament{lily}\,\florilegiumornament{butterfly}\,\florilegiumornament{bee}\,\florilegiumornament{bird}\,%
\florilegiumornament{hedera}\,\florilegiumornament{sparkle}\,\florilegiumornament{cyme}\,\florilegiumornament{umbel}.
