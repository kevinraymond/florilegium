How the inks read

    
Each ink's middle tone against the accent, as most readers see it and as Machado, Oliveira and
      Fernandes's model (2009) shows it for the three color blindnesses at full strength, measured as the
      distance between the two colors in Oklab. Under 0.10 they are hard to tell apart in a fine line
      (marked ☞); over 0.15 they are clear. Those two thresholds are this page's rule of thumb, not a standard.
